# THE RIFT — Act II

Design plan for the rift expansion. Chapters 11–16, following the current
campaign and its epilogue.

## Premise

Chapter 10 sealed the tear. The epilogue already says the seam never fully
closed and smaller fractures keep opening — Act II is the decision to stop
waiting for them and go to the source.

The campaign stops being a defence and becomes an advance. That inverts the
whole game: for ten chapters Neo Tokyo was the thing you stood in front of.
Now you leave it behind you, and every step forward is a step further from
the people you have spent the entire game protecting.

## What the player does differently

**You travel.** A fixed rift site sits far beyond the districts, visible on
the horizon from anywhere the way Fuji already is. Each chapter stages further
out. The city thins, then stops.

**The wards consolidate and follow.** Act II opens with Command emptying the
four wards into a single hardened staging shelter that relocates up the line
behind you each chapter. This is what makes leaving the city possible without
throwing away the shelter mechanic — capacity, overflow, the drain and
Kotetsu's expansion all keep working, on one ward instead of four. Losing it is
still the run.

**Corruption scales with proximity.** One scalar, distance to the rift,
drives sky and fog tint, ground corruption, enemy density, and music
intensity. Everything gets worse the closer you get, and it does so smoothly
rather than per-chapter, so the approach is felt continuously.

**Encounters get crowded.** Act II drops the one-boss-per-chapter format.

| Ch | Staging | Encounter |
|----|---------|-----------|
| 11 | Edge of the districts | Two bosses at once |
| 12 | Beyond the bay | Two bosses + heavy swarm |
| 13 | The dead ground | Two bosses, corrupted variants |
| 14 | The approach | Two bosses + rift-spawn swarms |
| 15 | The mouth | Three at once — a gauntlet |
| 16 | Inside the seam | REVENANT, alone |

Corrupted variants reuse the existing ten bosses with the phase system already
in place: they start in phase 2, hit harder, and are re-skinned by the
corruption tint rather than being new models.

## REVENANT — TA-00

The dark Terra-Armor. Rei's suit, lost three years ago, that went into the
rift and came back under power.

**It is not a copy of your suit — yours is the copy of it.** The prologue
already calls the player's suit a prototype. Rei flew the one before it.
Kurosawa gets to deliver that, and it lands as a small twist of its own.

Fight design, in order of how much it matters:

- **It mirrors your kit.** Saber combo, rifle, dash, beam, nova — whatever you
  have unlocked, it has. Fighting it is fighting the moveset you have spent
  ten chapters mastering.
- **It adapts.** It tracks which weapon you lean on and builds resistance to
  it, forcing rotation through the wheel. This is what finally makes nine
  weapons matter instead of picking a favourite and holding the trigger.
- **It dashes out of combos**, so lock-on-and-hold does not work on it.
- **Three phases and punish windows**, built on the boss system already
  shipped — but its windows are short and it recovers fast.
- **Phase 3 it stops fighting like a machine and fights like Rei** — the
  pattern from MEMORIES, the one Aya says Rei used to fly. Aya recognises it
  over comms mid-fight. The gut-punch arrives through mechanics, not a
  cutscene.

## The twist, and how it is seeded

The copy has every memory up to the instant Kuroki called the turn, and
nothing after. It is frozen there. It believes it was followed into a
manoeuvre and then left to die, and it has believed that, alone, for three
years of a life it does not know it is not living.

Seeded across the act rather than dumped at the end:

- **Ch 11** — Kurosawa reads a Terra-Armor signature in the rift. Assumed to
  be debris from three years ago.
- **Ch 12** — The debris is moving. Under power.
- **Ch 13** — Comms bleed. A fragment of Rei's voice, in the archived cadence,
  saying something that is *not* in the archive. Aya stops transmitting.
- **Ch 14** — It shadows the fight and refuses to engage. Hinata: *"It looked
  at you, senpai. Only you."*
- **Ch 15** — It speaks live for the first time, and asks the question:
  *"You called the turn. I followed it. Where were you?"*
- **Ch 16** — The fight. Phase 2 it realises it does not know what year it is.
  Phase 3 it asks Aya to confirm the shelters are clear — the last thing
  archived-Rei ever said — and Aya has to answer it.

**The ending.** It cannot be saved; there is no version of it with a future,
because it has no memory after the turn. What it can be given is the answer it
has been owed. Kuroki tells it the truth he has never said out loud: he did go
back, and there was nothing to go back to. Aya — who was on comms, who heard
the signal go quiet, who has spent three years not saying it — gets to say it.
Then it stands down, and the seam takes it.

Rei still died three years ago. Nothing undoes that. The act is about the
sentence that never got finished, not about getting her back.

## Build order

Three stages, each independently shippable.

**Stage 1 — the road.** Rift site in worldgen, corruption scalar, sky and
terrain shift, ward consolidation and relocation, chapters 11–15 with
multi-boss encounters. Fully playable without the Revenant; de-risks the
journey systems, which are the biggest unknown.

**Stage 2 — the Revenant.** The entity itself: mirrored moveset, adaptive
resistance, three phases, the Rei pattern in phase 3.

**Stage 3 — the twist.** The full dialogue arc, the comms bleed, the ending
beat, and a new epilogue feeding into endless mode.

## Notes

- Rei is Aya's older sister, Kuroki's wingwoman and his ex. This is consistent
  with every existing line and does not change.
- Endless mode moves to after chapter 16.
- New enemy class worth adding in Stage 1: rift-spawn, ground swarmers that
  only exist near the seam, to make "lots of monsters" read as a different
  kind of pressure rather than just more drones.
